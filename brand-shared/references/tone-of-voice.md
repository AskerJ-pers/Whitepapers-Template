## Writing rules

Document your non-negotiable copy rules. Some structural rules the system enforces regardless of brand:

- **Sentence case** on all headings, subheadings, and bullet points
- **No em dashes** — restructure sentences or use a comma instead
- **No exclamation marks**
- **Numerals** for all numbers (never spell them out)
- **Numbers are never zero-padded** — use "1", "2", "3" not "01", "02", "03"
- **No bullet points ending with a full stop**
- **No pronoun + verb contractions** in short-form copy or CTAs

## Pre-output checklist

Before writing any copy for a slide, check:

- [ ] Sentence case applied to all headings and bullets
- [ ] No em dashes
- [ ] No exclamation marks
- [ ] Numbers written as numerals
- [ ] All statistics attributed to a named source
- [ ] Tone matches audience (external: persuasive / internal: direct and informative)
